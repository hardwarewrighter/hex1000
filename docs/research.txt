No, do not snap a pin off to insert into that offset single hole.

The board features 19 main through-holes aligned on standard 
2.54 mm grid pitch on each edge. You should trim or snap the provided 20-pin headers down to 19 pins so they fit the linear rows cleanly.

Why that offset hole exists
Alternative Connector Footprints: That offset hole (along with adjacent pads) is placed specifically for auxiliary connectors, surface-mount components, or specialized test points (such as JTAG signals, external crystal pads, or JST battery/power connectors).

Breadboard Grid Alignment: The 19-pin side rows align directly with the standard 
0.1 in (
2.54 mm) grid of solderless breadboards. Bending or routing a pin out into that staggered hole would distort the header, prevent the board from sitting flush, and risk shorting neighboring traces.

Recommended Steps
Snap or cut off the extra 20th pin from each strip so you have two 19-pin header strips.

Insert the 19-pin strips into the main side rows.

Solder the headers in place. The board will plug cleanly into a standard breadboard without issue.
